Somebody has to be awake at 03:00.

Lamplight is a crew of five agents that works your backlog between midnight and dawn. They sort it, watch it, mend it, read it, and leave it on your desk before you're up.

Five of them, and each one has a job you can name.

  • Wren

    Triage

    Opens everything that came in overnight and decides what can wait until you are up.

  • Moth

    Watch

    Sits with the alarms all night, so the phone on your bedside table stays quiet.

  • Beetle

    Repair

    Finds the test that broke at 02:00 and has the build green again by 04:00.

  • Owl

    Research

    Reads the eight hundred page thing and leaves you the six lines that matter.

  • Hare

    Dispatch

    Packs the night's work, labels it, and puts it on your desk before you are awake.
